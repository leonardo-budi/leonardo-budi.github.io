About

I care about the details no one sees.

I'm Leo, a Senior Product Designer with 12 years of experience designing fintech and consumer products. I enjoy solving problems that sit beneath the interface, where product decisions, technical constraints, and implementation come together.

My work often involves finding the simplest direction that balances user needs, business goals, and what a team can realistically build, then making sure that thinking survives the journey from design to production.

I've worked across mobile fintech products, foreign exchange, payments, merchant products, and design systems. I'm especially comfortable working closely with product and engineering teams, where the best solution often comes from understanding the constraints as much as the problem itself.

Currently based in Jakarta, Indonesia. Open to Senior Product Design opportunities across Europe and remote international teams, with relocation available.

How I work

The principles behind my work.

01
Thoughtful Decisions
I don't jump to solutions. I spend time understanding the problem, questioning assumptions, and exploring different directions before committing to one. Every design decision should have a reason that can be explained and defended.
02
Intentional Craft
I care about how a product feels as much as how it works. Every interaction, hierarchy, and detail should make the product easier to understand. Good craft isn't decoration. It helps people know what to do next.
03
Pragmatic Under Constraints
Every project has trade offs. Tight timelines, technical limitations, and changing priorities are part of the job. I focus on making decisions that move the product forward without compromising what matters most.
