About

I care about what ships.

I'm Leo, a Senior Product Designer with 12 years in fintech and consumer apps. I care as much about how a product works underneath as how it looks: the systems, the handoff, and whether what ships matches what was designed.

I stay close to engineering, catching constraints early and keeping the gap between Figma and production small. I've owned a currency exchange feature used by 120,000+ people and built design systems from scratch. Based in Norrköping, open to senior roles across Europe or fully remote.

Strengths

What you actually get.

01
Systems Thinker
I don't just design screens. I think about how components connect, how teams use them, and how they hold up six months after launch. I've built design systems from scratch three times. Each one taught me something the previous one didn't.
02
Close to Engineering
I've spent most of my career working directly alongside engineers. I know what a good handoff looks like, I speak the same language, and I stay involved until the thing actually ships, not just until Figma is done.
03
Calm Under Constraints
Tight timelines, backend dependencies, briefs that change halfway. I've shipped work I'm proud of inside all of them by making the pragmatic call and knowing which corner is safe to cut.
